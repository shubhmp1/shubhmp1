## ⭐ Featured Project: Dual-ESP32 RC Car (Semi-Auto & Manual)

<!-- Add a photo or demo GIF of the car here, e.g.
<p align="center"><img src="car.gif" width="400"></p> -->

A remote-controlled car I designed and built end to end: custom PCBs, RF firmware, and a 3D-printed steering system, with a semi-autonomous driving mode.

![C/C++](https://img.shields.io/badge/C%2FC%2B%2B-00599C?style=for-the-badge&logo=cplusplus&logoColor=white)
![ESP32](https://img.shields.io/badge/ESP32-000000?style=for-the-badge&logo=espressif&logoColor=white)
![Altium](https://img.shields.io/badge/ALTIUM-F39C12?style=for-the-badge)
![nRF24L01](https://img.shields.io/badge/nRF24L01%20RF-2E7D32?style=for-the-badge)
![CAD](https://img.shields.io/badge/CAD-DA2128?style=for-the-badge)

- 🔧 **Hardware:** designed and hand-routed 2 custom PCBs in Altium, keeping the motor away from the RF module to limit noise
- 📡 **Wireless:** nRF24L01 link over SPI (2.4 GHz) with ACK-payload telemetry back to the remote, replacing Bluetooth
- 🎮 **Control:** 12-bit ADC joystick → deadzone → quadratic curve → slew-rate limiter → PWM to an L298N driver
- 🤖 **Semi-auto mode:** VL53L1X ToF sensor for distance braking and TCRT5000 IR sensors for lane-keeping, with live readings on a TFT
- 🛡️ **Safety:** mode-select state machine with debounced inputs and a safe-stop packet on every transition
- 🛞 **Mechanical:** Ackermann-style steering linkage in CAD, iterated across 3 wheel-rim revisions and 2 chassis/steering prints
